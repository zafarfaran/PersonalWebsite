Privacy: the trainers on faranz.com

This covers the chess, mental maths, machine learning and trading trainers. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Without an account

Your progress, notes and drawings are kept in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.

With an account

You can sign in with Google so that your progress is saved and follows you between devices. When you do, the server stores:

  • from Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account id. Nothing else is requested, and your Google password is never seen;
  • from the trainers: your progress, review schedule, notes, drawings, saved games and settings;
  • a sign-in token, kept in your browser, so you stay signed in for up to 90 days.

This is used only to save and restore your progress. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The data is held in a database hosted by Railway, and each account can read only its own rows.

Deleting your data

Open the account button in the corner of any trainer and choose "Delete my account and saved progress". That removes everything the server holds about you, at once. Progress in your browser stays until you clear it.
